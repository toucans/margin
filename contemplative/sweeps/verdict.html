Eight framings · who is already being sold to

Which framing has a market

A map of where money already moves, not a scorecard against one buyer. The buyer is still an open question — so the useful reading is: each framing has an audience the advertising picks for you, and they are not the same audience.

The answer, in one paragraph

The strongest money in this study is drinking as the off-switch and the body under stress — and both are sold overwhelmingly to women in midlife, not to founders. If the buyer is genuinely open, that is where the proven demand sits.

Founders are the thinnest of the viable lanes, not the richest. Eight advertisers across 163,508 ads. They run long, which says the lane works — but it is a narrow door, and choosing it means choosing the harder market on purpose. Worth doing if you want that buyer for other reasons; not what the money would pick.

And the framing with the best story — the 2 a.m. decision — has no visible buyer at all. Not a bad sign on its own. Just untested by anyone but you.

The framing picks the buyer, so this table is really a menu. Drinking gets you midlife women. Nervous system gets you trauma and parenting. Burnout named for a role gets you executives. Nobody is reaching a founder through the mechanism — they reach them through the habit or the job title. Whichever you lead with, that is the room that shows up, and it is hard to swap later.

One pattern that cuts across all of them. On LinkedIn the ads running 90+ days to senior people sell keynote speakers, private staff and equity. The ones selling inner work run 9 or 10 days. If you go after any professional buyer, the evidence favours cohorts with real start dates over an always-on funnel.

What this rests on

Meta is complete and measured — 291 queries, 8 framings, 6 countries, 163,508 ads, zero errors. LinkedIn is about a tenth done: the full run aborted on an Apify billing cap after five keywords, all from C1, so its column rests on that plus two small probes of the burnout terms. C2–C5, C7 and C8 were never swept there.

Two softer joints worth knowing. “Runs long, therefore profitable” is an inference, not a measurement — a good one, and the premise the study was built on. And the buyer labels in column two are my reading of who the copy speaks to, from the ad text; they are the least certain thing on this page.
