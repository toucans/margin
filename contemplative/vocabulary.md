# Vocabulary

How this market actually words itself. Counted from 3,240 Skool communities —
930 in Spirituality, Health and Self-improvement once manifestation and
devotional groups are removed. Numbers are how many communities use the word.

Not a style guide. A record of what buyers already recognise, so the offer is
legible without having to teach anyone a new language first.

---

This is not evidence the work doesn't sell — 70 communities sell it under other
names, at ordinary money. It is evidence that **the tradition is never the
pitch.**

## The one caution

Frequency is not effectiveness. Nervous-system language appears in 10 of the top
23 communities and 10 of the bottom 23 (Fisher p=1.00). It is how to be
understood, not how to win.
